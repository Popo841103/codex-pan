ABOUT CO-HUB

Rest upstairs. Connect over a good conversation.

CO-HUB brings together hostel stays, a café and craft beer in Bukit Bintang, Kuala Lumpur. We are preparing women’s capsules, men’s dorms and private rooms, while building a community for travellers, neighbours and people working on new ideas.

We also supply coffee, craft beverages, guest essentials and hospitality equipment to business customers.

Follow our progress on Instagram ↗

A few things to know.

Can I book a whole dorm?

Dorms are sold by bed. To use a whole dorm, enquire about booking all beds in the same room; the team must confirm the arrangement.

Which rooms have private bathrooms?

All women’s capsules use shared bathrooms. Men’s dorms have ensuite and shared-bath options. Private double rooms have their own bathroom and balcony.

Can I order food on this website?

For online ordering, scan the QR code at the café. Collect your food and drinks at the bar and pay at the front counter. The public menu link is for browsing. Café hours: 09:00–23:30, Malaysia time.

Are online booking and mobile check-in available?

Accommodation bookings are not open yet. You can enquire on WhatsApp. Mobile check-in is planned; instructions will be announced when ready.

When will accommodation open?

Accommodation is planned to open during Christmas 2026. Bookings are not open yet. Request updates for the exact date and booking arrangements.

Can I join activities without staying?

You can enquire about community activities without an accommodation booking. Dates, fees and participation arrangements will be confirmed before joining.

Where can I find arrival information?

See the Visit & guide page for our address, Google Maps directions and arrival information. Our café, food and craft beer are open now.

Is coworking open?

The coworking area is not open yet. Our café, food and craft beer are open now.
