THE BREWERY

Dream Brewing. Since 2015.

Dream Brewing began building an international-standard fermentation and distillation facility in China in 2015, with annual craft beer production capacity exceeding 1,500 tonnes. Following its acquisition of California’s Toolbox brewery, the introduction of R&D expertise from breweries including Goose Island, and more than 30 international collaborations, it developed over 250 craft beer recipes and received 38 domestic and international awards.

Old Driver IPA won a silver medal at the 2021 European Beer Star competition in Germany. Dream Brewing’s signature range focuses on IPAs and fruit-led craft drinks.
