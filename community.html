    Choose a date and time slot, and write a short introduction. We will review your request after you send it on WhatsApp. Approved slots are reserved. Please wait for us to confirm your time on WhatsApp. After approval, we will remind you of the time and ask for a poster. Your event appears below once the poster is uploaded.

    Please leave both a phone number and an email. If something comes up suddenly, we will call first, because email may not be checked in time.
