This page is only for you. After you approve a request, that time slot can no longer be booked. We then prepare a WhatsApp reminder so the host remembers the time and can upload a poster. The event appears on the guest page only after the poster is uploaded.

Where you get the message: After a guest submits, WhatsApp opens with a message for WhatsApp Business COHUB KL (+60 17-849 7319). It is not sent automatically. The same request also appears on this approval page for you to handle. After you approve it, another message is prepared to remind the host of the time and ask them to upload a poster.

WhatsApp Business COHUB KL is answered first by the AIWA assistant. Until this website can sync which slots are taken, the assistant only confirms that a request was received and leaves approval or decline to you. It will not approve a request on its own, and it will not tell the host that the slot is reserved.

Do not approve unhealthy or controversial content. Also do not approve anything illegal, harassing, or clearly unsuitable. If it is unclear whether the content is unhealthy or controversial, do not approve it automatically. Leave it for Xiaolin.

If a request has been with you for more than 24 hours and the event still starts more than 24 hours later (Malaysia time), the WhatsApp assistant may later decide using the review criteria. Auto-approve stays off until slot sync with this page is connected and confirmed, even when those 24 hours have passed, so a host is never told a slot is reserved while it still shows as free. If fewer than 24 hours remain before the event, do not decide automatically. Keep waiting.
